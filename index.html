Reading the numbers

Positive correlation
Yields and stocks rise together. Usually growth optimism: good economic news lifts both.
Negative correlation
Rising yields push stocks down. Typical when inflation or Fed tightening drives rates, as in 2022.
Per +10bp
The average index move when that yield rises 10 basis points (0.10%) over one period.
Inverted curve
2s10s below zero means 2-year yields sit above 10-year yields, a classic late-cycle signal.
